## Staged commencement (November 2025 notification)

MeitY published the DPDP Act enforcement timeline in November 2025. ONEDECORE records three stages:

1. **November 2025 — institutional and procedural provisions**  
   Specified institutional and procedural provisions under the DPDP framework commenced in November 2025.

2. **Rule 4 and consent-manager provisions — one year after notification**  
   Rule 4 and linked consent-manager provisions commence one year after notification of the DPDP Rules, 2025.

3. **Rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 and linked operational provisions — eighteen months after notification**  
   Rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 and linked operational provisions commence eighteen months after notification of the DPDP Rules, 2025.

## Readiness statement

**Designed for DPDP readiness; owner, operational and Indian legal-counsel review remain pending.**

ONEDECORE architecture is being shaped ahead of applicable commencement dates. **Compliance is not claimed.** Final operational compliance requires owner decisions, processor contracts, and qualified Indian legal-counsel review.

## Design principles tracked (not compliance proof)

See `DPDP_CORE_PRINCIPLES` in `legal-sources.ts`: independent notice, itemised data, specified purposes, purpose limitation, data minimisation, easy withdrawal, rights paths, processor contracts, reasonable safeguards, breach readiness, deletion when purpose ends, public privacy/grievance contact, and no dark-pattern consent.
